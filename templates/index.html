ℹ️ How It Works

AI Mode: A Q-Learning agent observes vehicle queues (N/S/E/W) and chooses which lane gets the green light. It learns to minimize total waiting time through trial-and-error rewards.


Fixed-Time Mode: Classic round-robin cycling — each direction gets equal green time regardless of queue length.


State: 81 states (3 density bins × 4 dirs)
Actions: 4 (choose green direction)
Reward: −(total waiting vehicles)
